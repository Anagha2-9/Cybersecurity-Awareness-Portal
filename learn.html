Digital Citizen Cyber Hygiene Pledge

Make an active commitment to uphold defensive cyber habits across your personal and academic accounts.

Why Individual Cyber Hygiene Matters

Over 82% of institutional data breaches originate from human error—such as reusing passwords, falling for deceptive phishing emails, or plugging in unverified USB drives.

The 5 Core Principles:

  • Password Uniqueness: Never repeat passwords across critical accounts.
  • Second-Factor Defense: Enable app-based 2FA on primary email and banking.
  • Hyperlink Vigilance: Hover before clicking; verify sender identity.
  • Offline Backups: Store critical coursework and records on detached media.
  • Software Hygiene: Apply security patches as soon as they are published.
