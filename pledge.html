Why Semantic HTML Matters

Before HTML5, web developers used generic <div class="header"> and <div class="nav">. Semantic elements explicitly communicate their informational role to assistive technology:

  • Accessibility: Screen readers announce landmarks like <nav> and <main>.
  • SEO: Search engine spiders prioritize text inside <article> over boilerplate.
  • Maintainability: Cleaner, more legible markup for development teams.
