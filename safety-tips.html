Comprehensive Digital Safety Checklists

Essential steps to verify your accounts, devices, and communications are secured.

🔐

Account & Auth

Protecting credentials and access gates:

  • Enable 2FA/MFA on all major portals.
  • Use 14+ character unique passphrases.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or auth codes.
  • Check HaveIBeenPwned for past leaks.
  • Revoke unused third-party app permissions.
🌐

Browsing & Wi-Fi

Navigating the public web securely:

  • Verify HTTPS padlock before logging in.
  • Use a trusted VPN on open public Wi-Fi.
  • Disable automatic Wi-Fi connections.
  • Hover over links to verify exact URLs.
  • Keep browser extensions to a minimum.
💻

Devices & Data

Shielding hardware and personal files:

  • Turn on automatic OS security updates.
  • Follow the 3-2-1 backup strategy.
  • Lock your screen whenever stepping away.
  • Encrypt laptops and external hard drives.
  • Avoid connecting unverified USB devices.
